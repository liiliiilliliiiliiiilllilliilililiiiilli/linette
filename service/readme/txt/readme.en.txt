This service:

	To provide the project with starting resources, I am creating the Linette VPN platform, where developers (providers) can publish their VPN connections and users can choose and connect – free or paid – bypassing censorship.

	This is not a VPN in the usual sense: it will not go down because of an attack by government agencies, and it will not steal your data itself, because it merely connects providers with clients.
	Providers earn income from subscriptions / ads, and users get a ranked top list of provider offers, including free ones.
	The platform itself takes a commission on paid connections.
